# 01 — Design Vision

## Product statement

Celestial is a native Android music player presented as an explorable fictional universe. It should feel like a universe that happens to be a music player—not a conventional music player with a space wallpaper.

All audio is read from one user-selected folder on the device. There is no account, stream, sync or network dependency.

## Core experience

The home screen is a fictional planetary system around an original, bright central star. The user can rotate the universe freely in three dimensions. Each destination is represented by a distinct planet. Selecting a planet triggers a cinematic camera journey into that world, where familiar glass UI presents the destination's content.

The currently playing song has a persistent miniature planet that remains visible while navigating between destinations.

## Confirmed visual identity

- Pitch-black space is the dominant canvas.
- A field of stars twinkles subtly.
- A bright fictional central star anchors the system.
- Imaginary planets—not the real Solar System—represent app destinations.
- Orbital movement is extremely subtle and almost imperceptible.
- User-controlled camera rotation is free in any direction.
- Planet labels are hidden by default and appear when a planet is approached or selected.
- Inside destinations, use minimalist, familiar glass UI over a deep-space background with stars and occasional comets.
- The first launch shows the universe being created through a Big Bang, star formation and planet formation. This animation resolves into the actual interactive home scene.

## Design principles

1. **Atmosphere first, clarity always.** The universe should be beautiful, but controls and content must remain immediately understandable.
2. **Spatial navigation, familiar content.** Planets are destinations; lists, search results, settings and controls use recognisable patterns.
3. **One continuous universe.** Intro, home, destination transitions and persistent player should feel like states of one coherent scene.
4. **Minimalism.** Avoid clutter, unnecessary labels, loud neon, decorative UI chrome and excessive motion.
5. **Performance is part of the aesthetic.** Stutters, laggy gestures or delayed playback destroy the illusion.
6. **Real functionality.** All visible controls must work and reflect actual playback/app state.

## Confirmed destinations

All major tabs/destinations are represented by planets:
- Playlists
- All Songs
- Search
- Favourites
- Recently Played

Settings is an exception: it opens as a floating glass panel over the current environment, not as a planet.

## Persistent music planet

The currently playing track is represented by a floating mini planet that remains visible across destinations. It uses the current album artwork as its surface and reflects actual playback state. Tapping it opens the expanded Now Playing experience.

## Visual tone

Heavenly, quiet, expansive, celestial, elegant, immersive and restrained. Avoid a cyberpunk aesthetic, over-saturated neon, busy sci-fi HUDs and generic dashboard styling.

## Open decisions

The exact fictional planet names, final planet-to-destination mapping, detailed visual identity of each planet, precise screen layouts for All Songs/Search/Favourites/Recently Played, onboarding permissions flow, and exact typography choices are not yet locked. Do not present proposals as confirmed requirements.
